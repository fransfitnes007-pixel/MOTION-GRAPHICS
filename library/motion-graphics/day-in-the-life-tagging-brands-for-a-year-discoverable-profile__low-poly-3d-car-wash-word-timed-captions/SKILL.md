## How it works
Same structure as parts one to forty three. New here:

- **Brushes** are a core cylinder with a ring of thin boxes, spun on their own axis; a `spin` value ramps them up as he walks in. Thirty foam puffs orbit and rise during the brush beat; a foam group on his head stays visible from then on.
- **He is hidden during phone sheets** (`P.root.visible = docMode < 0`), so his boots do not poke out under the sheet.
- **The flame** is drawn with bezier curves on canvas, so no emoji font is needed in the renderer.
