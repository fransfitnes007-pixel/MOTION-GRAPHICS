## How it works
Same structure as parts one to forty six. New here:

- **`makePerson(colours)`** builds any number of rigs and `applyPoseTo(rig, pose)` poses each one, so the students reuse the teacher's KNOT pose with their own wobble.
- **The knot** is one pose: the root tipped back (rx -1.25) and turned side-on, both hips folded past vertical so the legs go over the head, arms wrapped over; a sine wobble on the knees, arms and head makes her struggle.
