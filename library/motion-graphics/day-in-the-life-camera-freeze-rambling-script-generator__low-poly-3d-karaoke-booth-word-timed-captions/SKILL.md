## How it works
Same structure as parts one to thirty eight. New here:

- **The wall screen** is a canvas texture redrawn every frame: lyric line with a yellow wipe, optional SCORE box, optional REC.
- **The ring light** turns from grey to white, its point light comes on and the phone's dot goes red whenever she is recording; the ring camera sits above and to the side so the ring frames her chin without the phone covering her face.
- **Strangers** are simple box people whose arms swing up over their ears with one `ears` value.
