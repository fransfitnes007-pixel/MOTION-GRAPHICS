## How it works
Same structure as parts one to thirty nine. New here:

- **The clock face** is a canvas drawn once with the numerals mirrored (it is seen from inside); the hands are boxes in groups that turn with time and spin up with a `spin` value during the "whose day" beat.
- **Gears** are a cylinder plus box teeth; wall gears turn about their axis, the floor gear turns flat, and in the low point he lies on it and turns with it.
- **The chat** scrolls with a clipped `translate` so the later bubbles stay above the caption zone.
