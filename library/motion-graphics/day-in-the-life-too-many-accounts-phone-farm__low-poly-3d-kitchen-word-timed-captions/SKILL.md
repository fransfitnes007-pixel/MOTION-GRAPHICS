# Day in the life, part three: 18 accounts, one phone (low-poly 3D)

**Code name:** three.js kitchen and box-built character, a second dark room with a 4 by 5 rack of phones, hard cuts on voiceover word times, 2D canvas for captions, chips and the checklist. **From:** original build, part 3 of the Day in the Life series (`pipelines/day-in-the-life/`).

## How it works
Same structure as parts one and two (`state(t)` picks pose, camera, props and the 2D layer per beat). New here:

- **The phone farm** is built at x = 40, away from the kitchen: floor, back wall, shelves, 20 phone bodies each with its own basic material. Two shared canvas textures (`farmTexA` profile, `farmTexB` her video with Posted ✓). Per phone and frame: brightness (standby, on, account) and which texture, staggered by `order = column + (4 - row) * 1.3`. While it is on screen the kitchen's sun and sky light drop and the background turns near black.
- **Phone screens:** `drawPhone` has four modes (accounts list, login states, storage, the dog food post).
- **Props:** 14 app cubes on a ring behind her head, a box dog with a head that tilts and a tail that wags, a padlock and a drive for the last beat.
- `code/demo.html` is built from `code/demo.src.html` plus `code/_shared_ui.js`: replace `/*SHARED_UI*/` with the file.

## Sound
Voice: TTS voice A (see `_engine/VOICES.md`), sped 1.18x, take 1 of 2. `code/recipe.py` adds six rising dings for the yeses, ticks as the list fills, thud and buzzer on the stamps, two small barks, a low tone into the farm, ticks as screens wake and post. No music. Nobody has listened to the mix yet.

## Adapt it
New script: generate the voice, run `code/voice/words.py`, replace `WORDS`, regroup `GROUPS`, move `T` and mirror it in `recipe.py`. The product claims here are: YourBrand has a phone farm of real phones with its own accounts, the creator makes no accounts, films, uploads and submits, and the farm posts the video. Check any new claim before it goes in.
