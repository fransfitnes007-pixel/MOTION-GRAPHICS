## How it works
Same structure as parts one to six. New here:

- **Outdoor set:** no walls; a low parapet, eight towers sharing one lit-window texture (basic material, tinted by mood), a sun that rises as `good` goes to 1, cloud blobs, and `scene.background` lerped from grey-blue to peach. String lights are spheres on a sagging line of thin boxes.
- **Bandit mask:** `P.mask`, a band that sits just behind the eye boxes so the eyes read as holes.
- **Tumbleweed:** two wireframe icosahedrons that roll and bounce across the `wider` shot.
- **Pages:** `drawPhone` (link, second link, lock screen), `drawDoc` (0 "You need access", 1 the email with three bad files, 2 the content bank). Everything a viewer must read sits above canvas y 640, clear of the caption.
- The claim is narrow: this campaign has a content bank with the logo, screen recordings and app clips, in one folder shared with the creator. It does not say every campaign has one.
- `code/demo.html` is built from `code/demo.src.html` plus `code/_shared_ui.js`: replace `/*SHARED_UI*/` with the file.

## Sound
Voice: TTS voice A (see `_engine/VOICES.md`), sped 1.3x (this take ran slow: 49 s raw), take 1 of 2. `code/recipe.py` adds message tones on the links, thud and buzzer on EXPIRED and on each bad file, a long wind with soft bumps for the tumbleweed, REC blips, a ding per folder, a falling tone on "Campaign ended". No music. Nobody has listened to the mix yet.

## Files
`code/demo.html` (runnable), `code/demo.src.html` + `code/_shared_ui.js` (the build sources: `/*SHARED_UI*/` in the src is replaced by the shared file), `code/recipe.py` (synthesised sound effects on the beat times; drop your own voice take next to it as `voiceover.wav`), `code/three.min.js` (three.js, MIT), `code/brand-assets.js` (generated by `engine/tools/apply_brand.py`), `media/poster.jpg`. Voice audio, the mixed sound and the rendered clip are not published.
