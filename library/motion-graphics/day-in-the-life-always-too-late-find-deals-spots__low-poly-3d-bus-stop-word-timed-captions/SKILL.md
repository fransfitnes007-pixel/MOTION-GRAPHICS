## How it works
Same structure as parts one to thirty three. New here:

- **The bus** is one group moved along x: `leave(t0, d, far)` eases it out from the stop. The first departure is quick; the callback one is slow so the camera can follow it to the waving arm (`wave`, a group on the bus side that only shows for the callback).
- **Running:** `run(x0, x1, t0, t1)` returns a pose with swinging legs and arms, a bounce and a forward lean while moving the root along the pavement.
- **The street camera** looks down from high across the road so the stopped bus does not hide him; for the callback it slides along with the bus.
- `code/demo.html` is built from `code/demo.src.html` plus `code/_shared_ui.js`: replace `/*SHARED_UI*/` with the file.

## Sound
Voice: TTS voice B (men) (see `_engine/VOICES.md`), take 1 of 2 (raw 39.2 s), sped 1.1x. `code/recipe.py` lays street traffic under everything, then a whoosh and pop per message, a stamp for FULL, CLOSED and FILLED, footsteps for the runs, a hiss and an engine for each departure, the reveal chord, pops as the deals land, a ding for spots left, a click and two dings for Applied, swats, and two friendly tones for the wave. No music. The mux uses the limiter. Nobody has listened to the mix yet.
