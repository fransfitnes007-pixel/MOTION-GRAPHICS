## How it works
Same structure as parts one to forty five. New here:

- **Bees** are tiny groups (body, two stripes, two wing planes) on circular orbits round a home hive; a `swarm` value from 0 to 1 moves every orbit's centre onto him, shrinks the radius and speeds it up. Half the bees are hidden behind phone sheets.
- **The honeycomb** is a canvas of hexagons on a frame placed between his two hands each frame.
