# Day in the life, part forty nine: the cake photo (low-poly 3D)

**Code name:** three.js barn reception at golden hour (open door onto a low sun, string lights, round tables, a flower arch, a dance floor, a three-tier cake), four box-built people (bridesmaid, bride, groom, photographer), a bouquet on one arc, a squashing cake, floating phone sheets, hard cuts on voiceover word times. **From:** original build, part 49 of the Day in the Life series (`pipelines/day-in-the-life/`).

## How it works
Same structure as parts one to forty eight. New here:

- **People** come from one `makePerson(options)` (dress length, updo, veil, cap, shirt and tie, bare arms) and one `applyPose(person, pose)`, so four characters share the pose system.
- **The bouquet** flies one arc in world space from the bride's hand to the bridesmaid's face between two word times; the three shots that follow it only change the camera, so the flight stays continuous across cuts.
- **The cake** is a group of tiers that scales flat and wide when she lands, with icing splats switched on.
