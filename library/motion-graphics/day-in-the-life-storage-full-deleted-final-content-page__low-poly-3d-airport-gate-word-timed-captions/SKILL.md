## How it works
Same structure as parts one to thirty four. New here:

- **The lying-down low point:** the `LIE` pose rolls the root a quarter turn about z so she lies on her side along the seat row.
- **Doc sheets** draw a storage bar, deletion rows that shrink and spin away, an emptied bin, a thumbnail that grows into big pixels, the Content list with verdict chips, a draft player with a progress bar, and a grid of cat tiles (`catTile`).
- `code/demo.html` is built from `code/demo.src.html` plus `code/_shared_ui.js`: replace `/*SHARED_UI*/` with the file.

## Sound
Voice: TTS voice A (see `_engine/VOICES.md`), take 1 of 2 (raw 42.3 s), sped 1.14x. `code/recipe.py` lays a low hall hum under everything and a three-note airport chime, then a buzz and stamp for storage full, a trash swish per deletion and a low tone for the final, stamps for EMPTIED and THUMBNAIL, a thud for the seats, the reveal chord, pops and dings for the drafts, a click to play and two dings for "still there", swats, a run of pops as the cats fill up. No music. The mux uses the limiter. Nobody has listened to the mix yet.

## Files
`code/demo.html` (runnable), `code/demo.src.html` + `code/_shared_ui.js` (the build sources: `/*SHARED_UI*/` in the src is replaced by the shared file), `code/recipe.py` (synthesised sound effects on the beat times; drop your own voice take next to it as `voiceover.wav`), `code/three.min.js` (three.js, MIT), `code/brand-assets.js` (generated by `engine/tools/apply_brand.py`), `media/poster.jpg`.
