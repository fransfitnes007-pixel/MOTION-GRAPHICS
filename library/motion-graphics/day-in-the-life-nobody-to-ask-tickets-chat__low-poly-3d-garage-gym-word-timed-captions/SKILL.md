# Day in the life, part six: number 41 in the queue (low-poly 3D)

**Code name:** three.js garage gym and box-built character, a floating phone and page for the email, bot chat and campaign chat, hard cuts on voiceover word times. **From:** original build, part 6 of the Day in the Life series (`pipelines/day-in-the-life/`).

## How it works
Same structure as parts one to five. New here:

- **Hold:** `hold(t)` draws the queue card with `mmss()` for the racing timer; `floatNotes()` puts five note props on a ring; `fast` spins the wall clock.
- **The bot:** `bot` / `botHead` (box head, visor, blinking eyes, antenna) rises beside the phone in the chat scene and is the first thing swatted at the end. `botFace()` draws the same face as a chat avatar.
- **The campaign chat** (`drawDoc` mode 1) is a generic dark chat, not a copy of chat's interface: a channel title, team avatars with the G badge, his question, the reply.
- **Callback shot:** `drawPhone('hold')` with a timer that starts at 3:07:12.
- Wording follows the app: "Open a ticket and the team gets pinged in chat right away"; a private chat with the YourBrand team and the other creators on the campaign. No reply time is promised.
- `code/demo.html` is built from `code/demo.src.html` plus `code/_shared_ui.js`: replace `/*SHARED_UI*/` with the file.

## Sound
Voice: TTS voice B (men) (see `_engine/VOICES.md`), sped 1.16x, take 1 of 2. `code/recipe.py` adds a little holiday jingle on the auto-reply, square beeps for the bot, a ring tone, thin hold music under the queue and again under the callback, a buzzer on WRONG, a two-note ping. The hold music is the only music and is part of the joke. Nobody has listened to the mix yet.
