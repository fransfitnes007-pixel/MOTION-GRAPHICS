## How it works
Same structure as parts one to thirty two. New here:

- **The abacus** is a frame and five rods of five beads; `slide` counts up over time and each bead jumps to the far end in turn. It drops onto the table on "abacus", hides for the swat scene, and comes back for the callback, lifted a little so the caption does not hide it.
- **Doc sheets** draw the three apps' cards (one per word), a camera roll that fills with screenshots, a lined notebook with a crossed-out sum, the profile's handles (Queued, then Tracking) and the analytics cards.
- `code/demo.html` is built from `code/demo.src.html` plus `code/_shared_ui.js`: replace `/*SHARED_UI*/` with the file.

## Sound
Voice: TTS voice A (see `_engine/VOICES.md`), take 2 of 2 (raw 41.4 s), sped 1.12x. `code/recipe.py` lays soft birdsong and far traffic under everything, a pop per app and a low tone per unit, a burst of shutter clicks for the screenshots, pencil scratches, a low tone for the morning gone, a thud and bead clicks for the abacus, the reveal chord, dings as the handles start tracking, pops as the cards light, a rising slide for "updates", swats, bead clicks and a ding for "lovely". No music. The mux uses the limiter. Nobody has listened to the mix yet.
