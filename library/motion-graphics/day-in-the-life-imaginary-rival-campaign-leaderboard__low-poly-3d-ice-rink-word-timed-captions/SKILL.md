# Day in the life, part forty one: Brenda (low-poly 3D)

**Code name:** three.js ice rink at night cut away at the front (ice with lines and a circle, boards, stands, overhead lamps, a players' bench), a cardboard cutout drawn on a canvas, box-built skater with a ponytail, a red puffer vest and skates, floating phone sheets, hard cuts on voiceover word times. **From:** original build, part 41 of the Day in the Life series (`pipelines/day-in-the-life/`).

## How it works
Same structure as parts one to forty. New here:

- **Brenda** is one canvas drawn by `brendaTex()` on the front face of a thin box (side faces in cardboard brown); the same drawing makes the small swatted copy. She rises by scaling the group's Y and slides away along the ice in the race.
- **Skating** follows a `circle()` path with the root's Y rotation facing along it; the skating camera follows her position.
- **The rink lights:** half the lamp boxes are dark before YourBrand; a spotlight's intensity rises with `good`.
