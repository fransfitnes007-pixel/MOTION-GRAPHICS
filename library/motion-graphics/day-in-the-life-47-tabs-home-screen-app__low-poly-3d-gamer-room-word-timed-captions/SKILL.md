## How it works
Same structure as parts one to thirty five. New here:

- **The browser** (`browser(x, w, h, t, sc)`) draws the same tab bar on the monitor canvas and on the big floating sheet: the tab count rises with each item named, the tab width eases down to icon size, the upload tab disappears after the close, and after the turn it shows 46 tabs and the recipe page.
- **Spinning:** the chair group and the person's root share a yaw (`spin`) that keeps turning after "ninety nine", with the `LIMP` pose.
- **RGB:** the strips and the keyboard glow cycle hue over time; the blinds' slats tilt and lift open as the light comes up.
- `code/demo.html` is built from `code/demo.src.html` plus `code/_shared_ui.js`: replace `/*SHARED_UI*/` with the file.

## Sound
Voice: TTS voice B (men) (see `_engine/VOICES.md`), take 2 of 2 (raw 39.5 s), sped 1.1x. `code/recipe.py` lays a computer fan hum under everything with bursts of keyboard clicks, then pops per tab, a falling slide as the tabs shrink, a click and swish for the close, a stamp and low tone for 99%, a creaky chair spin, the reveal chord, a tap and rising slide for the icon, two dings for "signed in", pops for the tiles, swats, more keys, and a small tone for the recipe. No music. The mux uses the limiter. Nobody has listened to the mix yet.
