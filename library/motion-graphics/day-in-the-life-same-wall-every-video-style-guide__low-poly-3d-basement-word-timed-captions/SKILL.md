## How it works
Same structure as parts one to forty one. New here:

- **The wall face** is a group of white and black spheres plus a box mouth whose Y scale follows a `talk` value; the pupils drift.
- **Load-bearing:** a separate beam group drops and tilts with `sag`; fourteen small boxes loop downward as dust; the bulb flickers.
- **Thumbnails** are drawn by `thumb()` (background, a box figure, optional eyes and a label) for both the identical grid and the varied one.
