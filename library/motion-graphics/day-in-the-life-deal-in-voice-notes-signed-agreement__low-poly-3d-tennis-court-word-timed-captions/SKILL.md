## How it works
Same structure as parts one to thirty six. New here:

- **The ball machine:** `fire(ball, t0, dur, target, scale)` moves a ball from `MUZZLE` to a target on an arc; the seven-minute note is a ball scaled up 2.6× and slower; the barrage sends all nine at once.
- **The knock-down:** the `DOWN` pose tips her root back a quarter turn onto the court.
- **Voice notes** are drawn by `vnote()` (play triangle, waveform bars, duration) and reused in the chat, the scroll and the callback.
- `code/demo.html` is built from `code/demo.src.html` plus `code/_shared_ui.js`: replace `/*SHARED_UI*/` with the file.

## Sound
Voice: TTS voice A (see `_engine/VOICES.md`), take 1 of 2 (raw 43.4 s), sped 1.17x. `code/recipe.py` lays birdsong and a far rally under everything, a blip per voice note, a machine thunk and a racket pock per ball, a bark for the dog, fast scroll clicks, a barrage of thunks and pocks with a stamp and a thud for the fall, the reveal chord, a stamp for "in writing", dings for read, signed and countersigned, pops and dings for the campaign, swats, and two barks at the end. No music. The mux uses the limiter. Nobody has listened to the mix yet.
