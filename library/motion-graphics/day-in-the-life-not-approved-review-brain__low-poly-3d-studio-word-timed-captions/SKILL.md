# Day in the life, part two: not approved (low-poly 3D)

**Code name:** three.js studio and box-built character, hard cuts on voiceover word times, lumpy icosahedron brain with 12 nodes, 2D canvas for captions, stamps and the review card. **From:** original build, part 2 of the Day in the Life series (`pipelines/day-in-the-life/`).

## What you see
Render it: `node engine/tools/render_demo.cjs video library/motion-graphics/day-in-the-life-not-approved-review-brain__low-poly-3d-studio-word-timed-captions/code/demo.html out.mp4 1080 1920` (about 39.5 seconds, 1080x1920; no voice is included, see `pipelines/day-in-the-life/PLAYBOOK.md`).

1. **0 to 4.4s.** Evening studio: city window, pink ON AIR sign, softbox. He films with a product in his hand. "Part two."
2. **4.4 to 7.9s.** A big phone sends the video to the brand, a red NOT APPROVED stamp lands. Close-up: "No reason. Just no." A pill starts counting NOT APPROVED ×1.
3. **7.9 to 15.1s.** Films it again (×2). Changes the hook: he holds up a fish hook (×3). Changes his whole personality: pink jacket, cowboy hat, sunglasses, dancing (×4).
4. **15.1 to 20.1s.** On the couch from above, the window goes day and night three times, DAY 1 to 3. The chat: three of his messages on read, then "make it pop more". "What the fuck does that mean."
5. **20.1 to 22.9s.** The laptop opens, the lamp comes on, the room warms, the chrome monogram spins in. He uploads the video.
6. **22.9 to 29.3s.** The brain: a scan ring passes over it, 12 nodes light up, a Review brain card scans the video's timeline. Two red marks, their times, then two cards: 0:02 Hook is slow, 0:07 Show the app sooner.
7. **29.3 to 31.9s.** He fixes it, uploads again, green APPROVED stamp, arms up.
8. **31.9 to 34.5s.** He swats away a question mark and a clock: ✕ GUESSING, ✕ WAITING. Arms crossed: "No bullshit."
9. **34.5 to 39.5s.** Black end card: chrome monogram, wordmark, "Upload it. Fix what it says. Approved.", yourbrand.com.

## How it works
Same structure as part one: `state(t)` picks a pose, a camera from `CAM`, props and the 2D layer per beat. New here:

- **The brain:** `lobeGeo()` pushes icosahedron vertices in and out with sines (a function of position, so shared corners stay joined), two lobes, a cerebellum and a stem, 12 small spheres as nodes that switch on one by one, a torus scan ring. The room lights drop to 42% while it is on screen.
- **Rejection count:** `NA` holds the four stamp times; `slam()` sets the stamp and the shake, and a pill at the top counts them until YourBrand arrives.
- **Costume change:** the jacket material's colour, the cap, the cowboy hat and the sunglasses are switched per beat. The fish hook hangs from his hand: its rotation cancels the arm's.
- **Review card:** `review(t)` draws the timeline, the marks, the time chips and the two fix cards. The fixes are `CONFIG.fixes` and match the voiceover.
- `code/demo.html` is built from `code/demo.src.html` plus `code/_shared_ui.js` (captions, pills, stamp, coin, end card, lifted from part one). Edit the src and rebuild: replace `/*SHARED_UI*/` with the file.

## Sound
Voice: TTS voice B (men) (see `_engine/VOICES.md`), sped 1.12x, take 1 of 2. `code/recipe.py` adds thud and buzzer on each stamp (heavier each time), a ting on the hook, a clock for the three days, a scan sweep for the brain, chords on the G and on approved. No music. Nobody has listened to the mix yet.

## Adapt it
New script: generate the voice, run `code/voice/words.py`, replace `WORDS`, regroup `GROUPS`, move `T` and mirror it in `recipe.py`. Check any claim about the review against the product before it goes in the script: this one says only that the brain watches the video and says what to fix and where.
