## How it works
Same structure as parts one to forty four. New here:

- **The daily loop** is one function, `loop(u)`, that maps a phase in [0, 1) to five poses (set up, film, edit, post, pack) and the ring light's scale; the first day steps on word times, Monday to Wednesday run it on a timer.
- **The window** is a canvas texture redrawn only when day or night changes; night also dims the sun and hemisphere and brightens the pendant lamp.
- **Bikes** are built from tori, spoke boxes and `seg2()` tubes between frame points; the repair-stand bike's front wheel spins at a speed set per scene.
- **Her tee** is one material whose colour changes per day and per clip.
