## How it works
Same structure as parts one to thirty seven. New here:

- **The accordion:** `setAcc(s)` moves the two red ends apart and spreads six bellows folds between them; a sine drives `s` while he plays, and a slow one on the floor makes it wheeze.
- **The spice jars** keep their shuffled index and their sorted index and `lerp` between them with a hop when they move.
- **Portholes** share one canvas texture redrawn every frame: grey sky, rain streaks and dull water before YourBrand, blue sky, sun and bright water after.
