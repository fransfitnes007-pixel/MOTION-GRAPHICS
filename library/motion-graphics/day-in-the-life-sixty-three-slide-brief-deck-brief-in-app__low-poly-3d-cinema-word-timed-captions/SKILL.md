## How it works
Same structure as parts one to forty two. New here:

- **The big screen** is a plane with a 1120x830 canvas texture, rotated to face the seats; `drawScreen(mode, t)` draws each slide (title, logo, bigger logo, timeline, the sunset slide with the tiny rule, the founder's colour) and only redraws when the slide changes or animates. A point light in front of the screen takes the slide's tint.
- **Vertical framing:** a wide cinema screen does not fit a 9:16 frame, so the screen is 7 x 5.2 units and the deck camera sits high in the back rows so her afro puff is a silhouette under it.
- **The slide-58 push** lerps between two camera presets (`tiny` and `tiny2`) instead of the usual push, so the 13 px grey line becomes readable.
- **The folding seat:** her seat's cushion is its own group hinged at the back; the fold value rotates it and blends her pose into a folded one (legs up, arms up).
