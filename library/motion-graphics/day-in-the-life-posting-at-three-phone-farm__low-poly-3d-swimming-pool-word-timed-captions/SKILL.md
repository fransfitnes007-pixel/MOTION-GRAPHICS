## How it works
Same structure as parts one to forty nine. New here:

- **The pool** is a sunken box: a tiled floor at y -3 with lane lines, four tiled walls, a deck in four pieces round the hole, and a translucent double-sided water plane at y -0.12, so the same scene works from above and from below.
- **Underwater** sets `scene.fog` to a blue fog, turns on six additive light-ray planes and eighteen rising bubbles, and hides any swimmer that would block the camera.
- **Splashes** are a pool of seventy small boxes on ballistic arcs plus expanding rings; each beat pushes `[x, z, start, size]` into a list that `splashes()` draws.
- **The clock** has hour and minute pivots set from `setClock(h, m)`: 2:58 until "at three", 3:00 after, and again for the callback.
