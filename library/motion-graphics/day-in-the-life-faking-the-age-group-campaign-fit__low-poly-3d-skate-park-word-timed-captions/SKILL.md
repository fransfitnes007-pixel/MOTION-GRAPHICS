## How it works
Same structure as parts one to forty seven. New here:

- **The halfpipe** is two quarter circles of ten tilted boxes each, plus stepped skirts, decks and coping. `hpPoint(p)` maps one number to a point on the surface (flat, then up either wall) with its tilt; `onSurface()` stands any object on it along the surface normal. His drop-in, the board's escape up the far wall and the teen pumping back and forth all use it.
- **The fall** is a hard cut from the deck shot to a side view: the drop runs down the left wall with an ease-in, the board carries on at speed and flies off the coping spinning, and he arcs forward and lands on his side, rotated a quarter turn about z.
- **The cap** pops on at "wore" and spins one and a half turns to backwards at "backwards".
