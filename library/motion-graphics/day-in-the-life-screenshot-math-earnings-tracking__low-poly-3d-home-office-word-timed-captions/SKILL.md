# Day in the life, part five: an accountant with a ring light (low-poly 3D)

**Code name:** three.js home office and box-built character, a floating spreadsheet and a YourBrand posts page drawn on one canvas sheet, hard cuts on voiceover word times. **From:** original build, part 5 of the Day in the Life series (`pipelines/day-in-the-life/`).

## How it works
Same structure as parts one to four. New here:

- **Costume piece:** `P.visor` is a group on the head; `visor` scales it in on "math" and `visorFly` moves and spins it away in the last shot.
- **Screenshot beats:** `snapFlash(t0)` returns a white flash for each time in `T.snaps`; `snaps(t)` draws the counter pill and the fan of cards.
- **One sheet, two documents** (`drawDoc`): mode 0 is the spreadsheet (shared `grid()` also fills the laptop screen), with tab chips that count to 31 and, after `T.del`, the delete dialog. Mode 1 is the YourBrand posts page: live counters (`live` seconds since `T.tracks`), earned tiles, and a `scroll` that brings the payout card above the caption.
- **Money is never a number:** earned shows as "$ • • •" and the payout card shows no date. The wording matches the app: "Paste the link to your post", "views tracked automatically", the Earnings page.
- `code/demo.html` is built from `code/demo.src.html` plus `code/_shared_ui.js`: replace `/*SHARED_UI*/` with the file.

## Sound
Voice: TTS voice A (see `_engine/VOICES.md`), sped 1.18x, take 2 of 2. `code/recipe.py` adds calculator keys, a two-click shutter on every screenshot, cell typing, a run of ticks as the tabs fly, counters ticking on the posts page, a ding per earned tile. No music. Nobody has listened to the mix yet.

## Files
`code/demo.html` (runnable), `code/demo.src.html` + `code/_shared_ui.js` (the build sources: `/*SHARED_UI*/` in the src is replaced by the shared file), `code/recipe.py` (synthesised sound effects on the beat times; drop your own voice take next to it as `voiceover.wav`), `code/three.min.js` (three.js, MIT), `code/brand-assets.js` (generated by `engine/tools/apply_brand.py`), `media/poster.jpg`. Voice audio, the mixed sound and the rendered clip are not published.
