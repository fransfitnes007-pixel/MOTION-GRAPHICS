# Day in the life of a UGC creator (low-poly 3D)

**Code name:** three.js room and box-built character, hard cuts on voiceover word times, 2D canvas on top for captions, pills and the end card. **From:** original build, part 1 of the Day in the Life series (`pipelines/day-in-the-life/`).

## What you see
Render it: `node engine/tools/render_demo.cjs video library/motion-graphics/day-in-the-life-ugc-creator__low-poly-3d-rooms-word-timed-captions/code/demo.html out.mp4 1080 1920` (about 40 seconds, 1080x1920; no voice is included, see `pipelines/day-in-the-life/PLAYBOOK.md`).

1. **0 to 3.5s.** Bedroom from above, creator asleep. "Day in the life of a UGC creator." She jolts awake.
2. **3.5 to 6s.** Over her shoulder at the laptop: paper planes fly out of the inbox, a pill counts PITCHES SENT to 50. Cut to her face: ✕ 0 REPLIES.
3. **6.1 to 9.8s.** ✓ 1 YES, arms up. Then a close-up: ✕ NEW ACCOUNT REQUIRED.
4. **9.9 to 13.5s.** A big phone: New account, then a warm-up feed while the room flickers day and night and the day counts to 21, then a red SHADOWBANNED stamp.
5. **13.6 to 17.9s.** Ring light and tripod. REVISION 1, REVISION 2, REVISION 9 (by nine she is flat on the floor and the tripod has fallen).
6. **18 to 20.8s.** A ghost floats out of the window with her invoice. "Fuck this": she face-plants on the desk, seen from above, and the laptop shuts.
7. **20.9 to 24.9s.** The laptop opens, the room turns warm, the chrome monogram spins in. ✓ ACCOUNT MADE, ✓ CAMPAIGN JOINED.
8. **24.9 to 28.3s.** She swats away a paper plane, a phone and the ghost: ✕ PITCHING, ✕ NEW ACCOUNTS, ✕ CHASING INVOICES.
9. **28.4 to 34s.** A dark checklist card ticks on each line: Read the brief, Make the video, Get approved, Upload the raw video. She shuts the laptop: "And I'm done. No bullshit."
10. **34.1 to 40s.** On the bed, arms behind her head. White flash, black end card: chrome monogram, wordmark, "Read the brief. Make the video. Done.", yourbrand.com.

## How it works
One room is built once from boxes (bed, desk, chair, laptop, tripod, window, clock). `state(t)` picks, for the current beat, a pose for the character, a camera from `CAM`, which props show, and what the 2D layer draws. Cuts are hard, like the reference; each shot pushes in a little.

- **Character:** `makePerson()`; origin at the hips, groups for torso, head, shoulders, elbows, hips and knees. A pose is a flat object of angles (`P0` lists them); `mixP(a, b, k)` blends two.
- **Screens:** the laptop and the big phone are canvas textures redrawn each frame (`drawLaptop`, `drawPhone`).
- **Mood:** `good` goes 0 to 1 when YourBrand arrives and warms the walls, sun and window.
- **Captions:** `GROUPS` lists which words show together; each word pops in when said, the word being said is yellow.
- **Logos:** `G_LIGHT` and `WORD_LIGHT` are the light chrome marks for dark surfaces (`G_DARK` is the dark one).

## Sound
Voice: TTS voice A (see `_engine/VOICES.md`), sped 1.18x, take 1 of 2 (`code/voice/`). `code/recipe.py` adds the alarm, plane whooshes, dings, thuds on the stamps, a ghost slide, chords on the G, the done and the end card. No music. Nobody has listened to the mix yet.

## Adapt it
New script: generate the voice, run `code/voice/words.py` on the sped-up wav, replace `WORDS`, regroup `GROUPS`, move the times in `T` and mirror them in `recipe.py`. Colours, step names and end lines are in `CONFIG`. Only the tall layout has been tuned; a wide cut needs new cameras.

Render: `node engine/tools/render_demo.cjs video <code/demo.html> <out.mp4> 1080 1920`, then `python3 code/recipe.py sound.wav` and mux (scale in_range=pc out_range=tv, loudnorm -16).

## Files
