# Short-form principles

These are reasons, not a template. Work out what this viewer needs for this idea; the structure follows from that.

## Who it is for
Write to one person you could name, as if you were sending them the video. Put specifics in the subject (a $40,000 quote, lesson four, 2,140 ratings), not in a label for the viewer.

## The first seconds decide everything
- The viewer weighs value against time. Make the payoff feel big and the cost small. Naming the size helps ("three mistakes in 40 seconds").
- The hook is readable in one or two seconds, on screen and in the voice at once.
- Deliver something real by 5 to 10 s, not just a promise.
- No brand name, app name or call to action in the first 4 s. The product shows up where the story turns (10 to 15 s for an ad, later for a parable, and say why).

## Every line earns the next
Short form is judged line by line. Each line opens a small question the next one answers; open a new question before the last one closes (around two-thirds in is where people leave). Never fake a question and then deflate it. A line that is interesting but off the promise is cut.

## Concrete beats vague
Numbers, names, dates and objects you can picture. "Three years later it is still a note on his phone" beats "for a long time". Simple sentences, under about nine words on average; one idea per line.

## Pace and picture
- Narration: 150 to 180 words a minute (about 5 syllables a second). No dead air.
- Something changes on screen every 1 to 2 s. More beats in the script give the edit more places to cut.
- Answer the doubt where it appears ("no code", "free", "on your phone") in a short aside.

## For AI and software products
One feature per video, shown working, demo under 8 s, no feature tour. Show the result first, the how second.

## Honesty rules
No guaranteed results, no income or health claims, no fake reviews, ratings or screenshots, no invented user counts presented as real. Placeholder numbers in a draft are marked as placeholders. Facts about an app or brand come from looking at the live page that day, with the date.
