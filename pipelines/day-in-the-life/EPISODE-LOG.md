# Day in the Life of a UGC Creator: episode log

The first 50 parts, made with this pipeline for one product and white-labelled here (the product is `YourBrand`).
**Read this before you choose the next episode:** a new name and outfit on the same story counts as a repeat. The
"Already used" lists at the end are the quick check. Each part's code is in the library piece named under it.

Voices: odd parts voice A (a woman's voice), even parts voice B (a man's voice). Captions, cuts and sound sit on the
word times of the voice take.
